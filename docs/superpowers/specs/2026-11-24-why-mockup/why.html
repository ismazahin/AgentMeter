Analysis

Why each model performed this way

On paper these two models are close relatives, each with about three billion parameters, run on the same GPU against the same 20 flows. Where their results part ways, the paragraphs below trace the difference back to what this session measured and, where the measurements run out, to how each model is built.

Numbers¹ point to a value measured in this session, listed beside each model. Highlighted text with a D reference rests on the model's published design. It is a likely reason, not a measurement.

Qwen2.5 3B

Time

Qwen2.5 3B averaged 10.80 s per flow, close enough to Llama 3.2 3B that the gap could be chance1. Most of that time went into the Reason step, 4.06 s of it, the one agent asked to write a full explanation rather than a label. Perceive and Act took a little over 3 s each, and Decide, which only picks a class, barely registered at 0.27 s2.

perceive 3.26 s
reason 4.06 s
act 3.20 s

Memory

Yet Qwen2.5 3B got through each flow with 35% less working memory, 157 MB against 243 MB3, on prompts of near-identical length4. The explanation probably sits in how it handles attention. It shares its key-value heads aggressively, two per layer across 36 layers against Llama's eight across 28, so every token it reads costs it about a third of the cache memoryD1, D2.

Tokens

Going in, Qwen2.5 3B had an apparent edge on tokens. Its vocabulary is larger, 151,936 entries against 128,256, and a larger vocabulary usually turns the same text into fewer tokensD1, D2. That edge never showed up here. The two finished within 2% of each other, 3,401 tokens per flow against 3,3594, most likely because the bulk of every prompt is flow features5, long strings of numbers that either vocabulary splits in much the same wayD1.

Llama 3.2 3B

Time

Llama 3.2 3B was marginally quicker on average, 9.89 s per flow1, though not by enough to rule out chance. Its time was spread across the agents in much the same proportions as Qwen's, with Reason again the heaviest step at 3.71 s†2.

perceive 3.07 s†
reason 3.71 s†
act 2.86 s†

The slow flows

Where Llama 3.2 3B did stand out was on its worst flows. Its slowest 5% finished within 10.86 s, more than two seconds ahead of Qwen2.5 3B6. Its Reason answers kept to a tighter length, with 95% of them under 210 tokens†7, so no single flow dragged on. With only 20 flows, and no test on the slow tail alone, this is a pattern worth checking rather than a settled difference.

Memory

The flip side is memory. Llama 3.2 3B needed 243 MB of working memory3, most likely the cost of its more generous attention design, eight key-value heads in each of 28 layers, roughly three times the per-token cache of Qwen2.5 3BD2. Its total peak was higher too, 3,276 MB against 3,128 MB8, and its slightly larger weights, 3.21 against 3.09 billion parameters, probably account for part of thatD1, D2.

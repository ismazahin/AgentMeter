Benchmark the resource efficiency of LLMs on your network flows

AgentMeter measures how efficiently open-source language models run a fixed 4-agent analysis task: latency, GPU memory, tokens, cost and energy — plus accuracy as context when your data has labels. It is a measurement tool, not a threat-detection product, and it makes no security decision about your network.

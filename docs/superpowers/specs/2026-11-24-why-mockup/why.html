Qwen2.5 3B same speed, less memory

Where the time goes

Qwen2.5 3B took 10.80 s per flow M1. The Reason agent took the largest share, 4.06 s, because it writes the longest answer of the four steps M2. Perceive and Act took about 3.2 s each. Decide took 0.27 s, since it only picks one label from a fixed list M2.

reason 4.06 s
act 3.20 s

Why it needs less memory

While answering, it used 157 MB of working memory on top of its weights, 35% less than Llama 3.2 3B M3. The prompts were about the same length M4, so the input does not explain the gap. A likely reason is its smaller attention cache. It keeps 2 key-value heads in each of its 36 layers, so for every token it stores about a third of what Llama 3.2 3B stores Likely.

Why the tokens did not drop

It read and wrote 3,401 tokens per flow, within 2% of Llama 3.2 3B M4. Its larger vocabulary would usually split text into fewer tokens Likely, but most of each prompt is flow features, long runs of numbers M5. Both vocabularies likely split numbers in much the same way Likely.

Llama 3.2 3B steadier on hard flows, more memory

Where the time goes

Llama 3.2 3B took 9.89 s per flow, close enough to Qwen2.5 3B that the gap could be chance M1. Like Qwen, its Reason agent took the largest share, 3.71 s† M2.

perceive 3.07 s†
reason 3.71 s†
act 2.86 s†

Why its slow flows were shorter

Its slowest 5% of flows finished in 10.86 s, against 12.99 s for Qwen2.5 3B M6. Its Reason answers varied less in length from flow to flow, 95% of them stayed under 210 tokens† M7, so no single flow ran much longer than the rest. The slow tail is not tested on its own, so this is a pattern in these 20 flows, not a proven difference.

Why it needs more memory

It used 243 MB of working memory, the larger of the two M3. A likely reason is its attention cache. It keeps 8 key-value heads in each of 28 layers, about three times what Qwen2.5 3B keeps for every token Likely. Its total peak was also higher, 3,276 against 3,128 MB M8. Its slightly larger weights, 3.21 against 3.09 billion parameters, likely account for part of that Likely.

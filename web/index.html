AgentMeter — benchmark the resource efficiency of LLMs on your network flows

AgentMeter measures how efficiently open-source language models run a fixed 4-agent analysis task: latency, GPU memory and tokens, plus accuracy when your data has labels. It compares up to two models head-to-head on the same flows and the same GPU. It is a measurement tool — not a threat-detection product — and it makes no security decision about your network.

GPU backend offline. The benchmark server only runs while a GPU is rented, so Prepare and Benchmark are unavailable right now. Finished results you have a link to will open again when it is back.

1 · Prepare

Upload a CIC-IDS2017-format CSV or a raw PCAP, or give a direct https link. AgentMeter validates it, draws a bounded, representative sample across the whole file and applies its flow-selection rules. You get a small, downloadable prepared set.

2 · Benchmark

Pick up to two of the five study models. Each runs the same flows on the same GPU, one at a time, in 4-bit NF4. You get a head-to-head efficiency verdict, accuracy when labelled, and a PDF report that names the GPU it was measured on.

Prepare your network flows for benchmarking

Step 1 of 2. Give a CIC-IDS2017-format CSV or a raw PCAP, by upload or by link. Prepare validates it, draws a bounded, representative sample across the whole file, and applies the flow-selection rules. The result is a small prepared set you can download and then benchmark. This is data preparation for measuring LLM efficiency, not an analysis — AgentMeter measures models; it does not detect threats on your network.

The server downloads the file itself (up to 10 GB). Use a direct download link — Google Drive / Dropbox “share” pages are refused. Only public internet addresses are allowed.
